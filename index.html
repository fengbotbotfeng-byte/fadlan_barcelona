Chapter I

Datu Bolango & Putri Boki

Long, long ago, before the name Bolaang existed, the land was nothing but dense forest and a wide river with clear water. There lived a wise tribal chief named Datu Bolango.

Datu Bolango had an only daughter named Putri Boki. She was beautiful, clever, and deeply loved her people. However, Putri Boki had one taboo: she was not allowed to bathe in the Bolaang River during a full moon.

Tribal Chief & Daughter Datu Bolango and his only daughter, Putri Boki
Sacred Taboo Not allowed to bathe in the Bolaang River during a full moon

Chapter II

The White Dragon of the River

One full-moon night, Putri Boki grew curious. She sneaked off to the river. The moment her feet touched the water, the river suddenly swirled, a bright light appeared, and out of the water rose a white dragon, the guardian of the river.

3

Chapter III

The Dragon's Gift

“Child of man, you have broken your ancestors' promise. This river is not a place to play. Yet because your heart is pure, I will not punish you. Instead, I will give you a gift.” Words of the White Dragon

The dragon then gave her a glowing stone called Batu Bolaang. It said that as long as the stone was cared for, the village would always be fertile, harvests would be abundant, and it would never run short of water.

Putri Boki carried the stone home. From that time on, the village was named Bolaang Village, after the stone given by the dragon.

4

Chapter IV

Honesty & Greed

Yet there was a condition: Batu Bolaang had to be guarded by people who were honest and not greedy. If a leader became greedy, the stone would grow dim and the river would run dry.

5

Chapter V

Blessings of Togetherness

To this day, the elders in Bolaang still believe that when the river begins to recede during the dry season, it is a sign that the villagers must return to working together, being honest, and caring for nature—because the village's blessings come not only from the stone, but from how its people look after one another.

6

Closing

The Legacy of Batu Bolaang

The legend of Batu Bolaang teaches us that true prosperity lasts when leaders stay honest, people care for nature, and every member of the community looks after one another.

Sacred Stone Batu Bolaang — symbol of fertility, harvest, and clear water
Timeless Lesson Honesty, cooperation, and caring for nature bring lasting blessings
